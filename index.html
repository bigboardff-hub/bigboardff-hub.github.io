Which platforms does BigBoard support?

Sleeper, ESPN and Fleaflicker. Sleeper and Fleaflicker need only your username or the email on the account. A public ESPN league needs only its league ID; a private one needs you to sign in to ESPN.

My widget is showing an old score

iOS, not BigBoard, decides how often a widget may redraw — usually about every fifteen minutes, and less often when your battery is low or you rarely look at that screen. Tap the red circle at the right of the widget's header to pull fresh scores immediately. The app itself is always current: open it and pull down to refresh.

My widget is blank, or shows sample teams

A brand-new widget draws sample data until it has fetched once. If it stays that way, open BigBoard, confirm the board you picked still has leagues on it, then remove the widget and add it again.

How many leagues can I follow?

BigBoard is a free download and follows one league at no cost. Two leagues is a one-time 99¢ purchase, and unlimited leagues is a one-time $4.99 purchase. If you buy the two-league option first, upgrading later costs only the difference.

I paid already, on another device

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in BigBoard. Purchases follow your Apple Account, so anything bought with the same Apple Account restores for free.

Does BigBoard see my password?

No. ESPN sign-in happens in ESPN's own web page, and BigBoard keeps only the session cookies it hands back — in the iOS keychain, never in ordinary settings. Sleeper and Fleaflicker need no password at all. There is no BigBoard account and no BigBoard server.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything BigBoard knows lives on your iPhone, so removing the app removes all of it. To clear only the stored scores, use Settings → Clear cached scores.

What do the coloured dots next to a player mean?

Yellow is questionable, orange is doubtful, red is out or on injured reserve. A cyan dot means that player's NFL game is in progress. On a tinted home screen, where iOS throws colour away, the marks get heavier as the news gets worse instead.
