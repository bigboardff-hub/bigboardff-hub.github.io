Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

BigBoard shows your fantasy football matchups from the platforms you already play on. It has no servers. Everything it knows about you stays on your iPhone, and the only services it talks to are the fantasy platforms you choose to connect.

What BigBoard stores, and where

Everything below is kept on your device, in storage shared between the app and its home-screen widget. None of it is sent to the developer.

  • The leagues you add: league IDs, league and team names, your own team in each league, and the nicknames, colours and board layouts you choose.
  • Recent scores: the last scoreboard fetched for each board, so a widget can draw immediately instead of showing a blank while it loads.
  • Your ESPN sign-in, if you have a private ESPN league: the two session cookies (SWID and espn_s2) that signing in to ESPN produces, kept in the iOS keychain and never in ordinary settings. Sleeper and Fleaflicker need no sign-in at all.

You can remove all of it at any time by deleting the app, or clear cached scores from Settings.

What BigBoard sends, and to whom

BigBoard talks directly to the fantasy platforms you connect, from your device. It sends only what each platform needs to identify your leagues:

  • Sleeper: your Sleeper username, to look up your leagues and scores.
  • ESPN: the league ID, and for a private league your ESPN session cookies.
  • Fleaflicker: the email address or user ID on your Fleaflicker account, to look up your leagues and scores.

It also fetches public NFL game information (kickoff times, game clocks and scores) from ESPN's public scoreboard, and public player information from Sleeper. Those requests carry no personal information.

Each platform handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

What BigBoard does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No account with BigBoard, and no BigBoard server: there is nowhere for your data to be sent even in principle.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Purchases

BigBoard is a free download. Following more than one league is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. BigBoard never sees your payment details; it only asks Apple which purchases this Apple Account owns.

Children

BigBoard is not directed at children and collects nothing about them.
